Welcome to Currere

New to the game? The interactive tutorial teaches everything in about two minutes.

Currere — Rules

Goal

Bank tiles off your far edge, the edge on your opponent's side. Each banked tile is 1 point. First to 6 points wins.

Your turn

Take exactly one action with one of your units. You may not pass.

Units

UnitTilesSpeedCan attack
Single11enemy Singles
Runner2–3= tilesenemy Runners of the same size or smaller
Fighter2–31any enemy with lower strength (Single 1, Runner 0, Fighter = tiles)

Nothing can attack a Fighter-3; only a detonation removes it. A unit's type is permanent and stacks can never be split.

Actions

Move up to your speed through empty hexes, in any direction. You don't have to use all of it.
Stack onto a neighbouring friendly unit, at most 3 tiles. Two Singles: you choose Runner or Fighter.
Attack a neighbouring enemy you can beat. It is removed; your unit stays put.
Detonate a Fighter with an enemy next to it: it and everything on the 6 hexes around it are removed, yours too.
Bank a unit that could reach your far row this turn: it runs off the board and scores 1 point per tile.

On the screen

Tap a unit, then tap a green dot to move, a red target to attack, a blue-outlined unit to stack, or an arrow beyond the edge to bank. Tap a Fighter twice to see its blast, and a third time to detonate.

Game end

The game ends at 6 points, when a player has no tiles left, or when neither player can act. Then: more points wins; if level, more tiles on the board; if still level, the player who moved second. A player with tiles but no legal action skips the turn.
